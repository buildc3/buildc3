## 👋 Who we are

**BuildC3** is a community of builders and developers — the kind of people who'd rather ship something rough today than perfect something in their head forever.

We exist for one reason: **too many great ideas never ship.** People sit on ideas because they don't have the team, the time, or the momentum to get from *"what if"* to *"it's live."* BuildC3 closes that gap — bringing enthusiastic builders and developers together to take an idea from concept to working product, **often in as little as two weeks.**

<details>
<summary><b>⚙️ How to activate the snake animation (one-time setup)</b></summary>

<br/>

Add this workflow as `.github/workflows/snake.yml` in the `buildc3/buildc3` repo:

```yaml
name: Generate Snake
on:
  schedule:
    - cron: "0 0 * * *"
  workflow_dispatch:
  push:
    branches: [ main ]

jobs:
  build:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: Platane/snk@v3
        id: snake-gif
        with:
          github_user_name: ${{ github.repository_owner }}
          outputs: |
            dist/github-contribution-grid-snake.svg
            dist/github-contribution-grid-snake-dark.svg?palette=github-dark

      - uses: crazy-max/ghaction-github-pages@v4
        with:
          target_branch: output
          build_dir: dist
        env:
          GITHUB_TOKEN: ${{ secrets.GITHUB_TOKEN }}
```

Once it runs once, the image above will populate automatically and re-animate on every push.

</details>
